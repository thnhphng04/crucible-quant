# Plan: instrument- and direction-specific strategies for Crucible Quant

Status: implementation plan, not approval for live trading. Requirements frozen on 2026-09-25. The Vietnamese counterpart is `PLAN-PER-INSTRUMENT-STRATEGIES.vi.md`; both documents carry the same decisions and acceptance criteria. The coding agent must read `CLAUDE.md`, current architecture and ADRs before editing. Do not read, list or copy root `holdout/` or `holdout.lock`; do not hand-edit `config/evaluation.lock.yaml`; do not delete or reset the ledger.

## 1. Goal, boundaries and settled decisions

Move from one source and parameter set shared across five spot pairs to independent search for each **`(instrument, direction)`** on Binance USDT-M perpetuals. Initial instruments: BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, BNBUSDT, XRPUSDT; daily signal timeframe. Select at most **one long strategy and one short strategy per instrument**, each with its own parameters. Both sides may be open simultaneously in hedge mode. Positions share one USDT account equity; margin is **isolated** by position side. At most 10 positions may be open.

Each position targets a price-move loss to its stop of **1% of account equity at entry**; the sum of risk budgets on open positions, pending orders and new entries may not exceed **10% of equity** when admitting a new entry. Freeze the stop relative to the filled entry; never widen it, scale in or automatically add isolated margin. Choose the lowest integer leverage that provides sufficient margin, only within the applicable bracket and with adequate liquidation-to-stop clearance. Leverage is not unlimited. Fees, funding, gaps and slippage may make realized loss different from 1%; report them separately.

Delivery covers perpetual data, GP/random search, all gates, joint-account portfolio, calibration, freeze/holdout, API/UI and reporting. No live-money execution adapter. The first campaign has **600 total trials**: 5 instruments × 2 directions × 2 engines × 3 seeds × 10 trials. It is a statistical smoke/pilot run; do not infer a per-scope GP win from ten trials per engine/seed. Calibration trials are outside the 600-trial evolution budget and must be recorded in the ledger. Do not run extra real campaigns to tune the policy.

## 3. Data contract and legacy compatibility

1. Add immutable `scope = (market_type, instrument, direction)` to candidate/trial/gate/event/member. `direction ∈ {long, short}`; use canonical contract IDs such as `BTCUSDT`, not spot pair strings as contract IDs. A portfolio slot is `(instrument, direction)` with at most one member. The portfolio hash includes source-hash/parameter mapping, data manifest, cost/funding model, execution version and risk policy. Lock a new protocol version before the first trial; retain v4 hash and semantics.
2. Perpetual trade OHLCV, 1-minute mark-price OHLCV, funding events/rates, instrument filters and leverage brackets require manifests with checksum, coverage and source. Daily bars generate signals; 1-minute data resolve stops/liquidation. Do not use v4 spot bars or replace missing funding with zero. All instruments require one valid common IS window and warm-up; missing coverage fails preflight before opening a campaign. Applying a current bracket snapshot to historical data must be labeled an **assumption**, not a historical reconstruction.
3. Keep perpetual holdout data separate from the existing spot holdout; create it through the project's permitted write-once/hardening path without opening holdout data during development. Do not reuse or overwrite the old lock/manifest. Missing OOS funding/mark data makes the evaluator fail closed, never PASS.
4. Use additive migrations; do not edit/delete existing trials, variants or campaigns. UI distinguishes `legacy_spot` and `usdt_m_perpetual`; new scope filters apply only to new campaigns. Add regression tests reading the original v4 report and migration tests against a temporary copy of the ledger, not destructive operations on the real ledger.

## 4. Signals, sizing, margin and execution

**Signals.** Grammar generates independent long/flat and short/flat strategies; validation rejects a wrong-side signal. Both strategies on one instrument run independently and may be open together. Compute signals on closed daily bars and enter at the next open under the established fill convention. `flat` closes only that strategy's side. A same-side signal cannot scale into an open position. After a stop/liquidation, consider re-entry only after the next daily bar close.

**Risk.** For an entry batch, snapshot joint equity `E` and set `R = 0.01E`. Given stop distance `d > 0`, compute `Q_raw = R/d` for a linear USDT contract, then round **down** to contract step. Validate quantity/notional bounds and tick; if rounding puts effective risk more than 1% below `R`, reject with a reason rather than claiming full risk allocation. Stop is filled entry minus `d` for long and plus `d` for short; round the stop **away from entry** to tick and recheck risk. Never change the stop after entry. Pre-fill sizing is provisional; recheck actual fill price, risk, margin and liquidation after fill; if invalid, close immediately under the model and record failure. Do not read the future open when producing the signal.

**Admission.** Before a new entry, sum stop-risk commitments of open positions and pending orders plus the new `R`; admit only when ≤`0.10E`. If falling equity makes existing exposure exceed 10%, block new entries and report the breach; do not move stops or arbitrarily close old positions. For one timestamp, process exits/stops/liquidations/funding first, snapshot equity, then process entries in canonical instrument order with long before short; lock this ordering in the protocol. All entries in that batch use the same `E` and `R`.

**Margin.** Calculate notional, bracket-specific initial/maintenance margin, fee reserve and free USDT. Choose the lowest integer leverage that funds the new position within the bracket limit. Leverage and margin type are symbol-level settings: while one side is open, retain the symbol leverage and admit the opposite side only if it fits at that leverage; never silently change leverage on an open position. Model isolated wallets and liquidation independently for LONG/SHORT while sharing account free USDT. For each side, liquidation must lie beyond the stop by at least **25% of entry-to-stop distance** in the adverse direction; 25% is the initial protocol default, frozen before the campaign. Reject with a coded reason when margin, bracket, clearance or metadata is insufficient. Do not arbitrarily shrink `Q` to evade the rule.

**Costs and price path.** Charge taker fees/slippage on entry and exit and timestamped funding separately by side; use mark prices for liquidation. If a 1-minute bar may hit both stop and liquidation without known order, use the worse outcome and flag ambiguity. Execute gaps through a stop at available prices and report realized loss above planned loss. Preserve the account drawdown kill switch. The new BacktestResult contract carries account equity, per-slot PnL/fee/funding, fills, stops/liquidations, denied orders and reasons.

## 5. Portfolio selection, gates and engine comparison

- Keep existing gate ①–④ laws/thresholds, applied to one contract/direction. A PBO grid never mixes candidates across scopes; all backtests and param optimization still enter the ledger. A runner failure is not a high PBO. Report measured/rejected/timeouts/missing-funding by scope.
- For each `(instrument, direction)`, choose the gate-④-passing candidate with positive IS Sharpe and highest `dsr_rank` in scope; break ties by lowest trial ID. Leave a slot empty if none qualifies. Do not reselect based on consolidated DSR. Build **separate GP and random control portfolios** with the same rule; do not select the winner engine independently per slot after viewing outcomes.
- DSR gate ⑤ uses returns from **one joint-account replay**, never a sum of standalone normalized trial returns. Report correlation/concentration, but do not filter slots post-selection because that changes the one-position-per-slot contract. Gate ⑥′ stresses fees/slippage and second source using the same runner, sizing, margin and funding. Calibrate at most once per member under the existing rule; if confirmation fails gate ④, empty the slot rather than picking a post-hoc replacement.
- Freeze only a fully validated portfolio hash/manifest in a research campaign before holdout. The holdout evaluator uses the same mapping, parameters, stop-risk, leverage/margin and costs on write-once OOS data. Do not revise rules because of v4 or pilot results.
- Comparison reports quotas and pass rates per `(instrument, direction, engine, seed)` and in aggregate; an aggregate GP/random decision requires a locked protocol and complete quotas. The 600-trial pilot marks individual scopes `exploratory` and assigns no per-scope win/loss; a 25-trial checkpoint monitor reports `insufficient_checkpoints`, not a conclusion of no divergence.

## 6. API/UI, implementation sequence and verification

**UI/API.** Add instrument/direction filters to candidate, archive and comparison endpoints; portfolio shows up to ten slots with source hash, trial ID, actual params, Sharpe/PBO and empty status. The chart API returns timestamps, account equity and `(instrument, direction)` PnL contributions after fees/funding. UI shows account equity/drawdown, per-instrument PnL with separate long/short lines, entry/exit/stop/liquidation events, planned risk, realized loss, leverage and skipped-order reasons. Starting equity plus contribution PnL must reconcile to account equity within rounding tolerance. An instrument chart represents **contribution**, not an invented subaccount.

**Sequence.** (1) Update architecture VI→EN and ADR, and identify exchange reference fixtures. (2) Write failing tests first for short, hedge, stop-risk, isolated margin, liquidation, funding and account accounting using synthetic data. (3) Add data/manifest and additive migration. (4) Scope scheduler/search/ledger and resume. (5) Add joint portfolio, calibration, gates and holdout fixture. (6) Finish API/UI, legacy regression and full quality checks. (7) Preflight real data and open a new pilot. Each stage can be reviewed separately; do not run real trials to debug code that has not passed fixtures.

**Tests/acceptance.** Verify ten simultaneous entries share one equity snapshot, each slot risks within ±1% after lot/tick rounding, total ≤10%, and an eleventh entry is denied. Test simultaneous BTC long+short and closing one without affecting the other. Cover bracket boundaries, insufficient margin, fixed symbol leverage with an existing side, liquidation clearance, both funding signs, stop gaps, ambiguous 1-minute bars and kill switch. Shuffled input must produce the same canonical admission; resume may not exceed quota; 600 trials distribute exactly ten per scope/engine/seed; one source hash on two scopes can calibrate independently. Verify account PnL reconciliation, old v4 API, no holdout leakage and protocol lock preceding trials. Run targeted pytest, CI suite, Ruff, mypy, lint-imports, documentation mirror and UI unit/e2e checks. Record actual results and gaps in the handoff.

**Stop condition.** Complete when fixtures demonstrate the joint-account path, migration/legacy reads work, UI reconciles PnL, and gates and holdout fixtures operate correctly. Run the pilot only after perpetual trade prices, 1-minute marks, funding and contract metadata have adequate locked coverage. If data is missing or the engine cannot credibly reproduce isolated hedge behavior, stop before the real campaign and report the exact blocker; do not silently substitute spot/cash execution or unbounded leverage.
